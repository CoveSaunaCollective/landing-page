Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar tools, such as local storage, do the same job in a different way. This policy explains which of these our website uses, and what the services built into our website may use.

The short version

  • Cove's own website does not set any cookies or save anything else in your browser.
  • Our website analytics work without cookies.
  • Our newsletter sign-up form uses Google reCAPTCHA to block spam, and Google may set cookies for that.
  • Because we only use what is strictly necessary, we do not show a cookie banner.

What our website uses

ServiceWhat it doesStores anything on your device?
PostHog (analytics, EU-hosted)Shows us which pages are visited, what is clicked and how people find usNo. It is set up to keep information in memory only, so nothing is saved once you leave the page.
EmailOctopus (sign-up form)Shows our newsletter form and sends your sign-up to our listWe have not seen it save cookies on our website.
Google reCAPTCHA (inside the sign-up form)Checks that sign-ups come from real people, not spam botsIt may set Google cookies, such as _GRECAPTCHA. These are strictly necessary to protect the form, so they do not need your consent.
Google FontsSupplies two of the fonts on our pagesNo cookies. Your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch the fonts, so Google receives your IP address.

Google reCAPTCHA is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Our link to Instagram is a plain link. Nothing from Instagram loads on our website unless you click it.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block Google's cookies, our sign-up form may not work. You can still join our newsletter by emailing info@covesaunacollective.com.

Changes to this policy

If we start using cookies that need your consent, for example when online bookings and memberships launch, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

Questions about cookies? Email info@covesaunacollective.com.
